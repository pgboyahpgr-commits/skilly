Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.0 · Updated 2 October 2026

This policy explains what Skilly does with your information. It is short because there is not much to say: Skilly has no servers, no analytics, and no advertising tracking of its own.

What we collect

Nothing, unless you choose to use an optional account or watch an advertisement.

  • Reading activity — which stories you have unlocked, your coin balance, your bookmarks and your reading position. This is stored only on your device using local browser storage. It is not uploaded, and it is never seen by us.
  • Your account — if you create one, it is provided by Puter. We receive only a username and an avatar reference. Their handling of your email address and authentication is governed by Puter’s privacy policy, not this one.
  • Device advertising identifier — when you watch an advertisement, the ad network (Start.io) receives your device’s advertising identifier so the ad can be served and measured. This happens inside the ad request and is not stored by Skilly.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or telemetry.
  • No crash reporting or diagnostic uploads.
  • No reading-history profiling or personalisation of content.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No advertising identifier in any form of our own.

Advertising

Skilly is supported by advertising. Ads are served by Start.io. When you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement, your device advertising identifier is shared with Start.io and its advertising partners for the purpose of serving and measuring that advertisement.

You can decline personalised advertising at any time from You → Ad privacy. Declining means advertisements will not be personalised, and advertisement-backed coin rewards will not be available. Declining does not remove any reading feature.

Your choices and rights

Where you have rights under GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA, or similar laws, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Because most of your data lives on your device rather than on a server, in most cases you can exercise these rights directly:

  • Delete everything on this device — clear the app’s storage, or uninstall.
  • Withdraw advertising consent — You → Ad privacy.
  • Delete your account data — delete it through Puter.
  • Opt out of sale/share (CCPA) — declining consent in the app sets a “do not sell or share” signal for advertising purposes.

Because we operate no servers and hold no user database, we cannot retrieve data we never received. For anything stored by Puter, contact them directly.

Children

Skilly is a general-audience reading application and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help resolve it.

Security

Because your data is stored on your own device, your security is largely a matter of device security. Keep your device protected with a screen lock.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will ship with an app update and the version number and date above will change. Questions about this policy should be raised through the contact channel published alongside the app.
