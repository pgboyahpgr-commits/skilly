How advertising works here

Version 1.0.0 · Updated 2 October 2026

This page explains, in plain terms, how Skilly makes money and exactly what you are agreeing to when you watch an advertisement.

Ads are labelled

Every advertisement in Skilly is marked SPONSORED or AD. Editorial content — the stories, the catalogue, and your own work — is never influenced by any advertiser. No advertiser can pay to change a rating, a review count, or the placement of a story in the catalogue.

Rewarded advertisements

A rewarded advertisement is one you choose to watch in exchange for something: unlocking a chapter, or coins. The rules are deliberate:

  • You always choose. Rewarded advertisements are never shown automatically. There is no “Watch now” button you cannot decline.
  • You always have an alternative. A gated chapter can be unlocked either by watching an advertisement or by spending coins. Coins can be earned without watching anything at all.
  • You only get paid for finishing. If you close an advertisement early, no reward is granted.
  • Free content stays free. Advertising never makes previously free content inaccessible.
  • There are limits. Advertisements are capped per session and per day, and full-screen interruptions are only ever shown at natural story boundaries — never in the middle of a chapter.

What the ad network receives

When an advertisement is requested, your device’s advertising identifier is shared with Start.io so the advertisement can be served and counted. We do not receive your identifier, we do not store it, and we do not use it for anything else. Start.io’s own privacy policy governs its handling: start.io/privacy-policy .

Turning it off

You can decline personalised advertising at any time from You → Ad privacy. Declining keeps the entire app usable — reading, search, saving, and Creator Studio all continue to work. You simply stop receiving advertisement-backed coin rewards, and advertisements you do see will not be personalised.

What you are never shown

Skilly does not show:

  • Full-screen advertisements at launch, or when you return to the app.
  • Advertisements in the middle of a chapter or between panels.
  • Advertisements on top of, or immediately after, a reward.
  • Fabricated reviews, ratings, read counts or popularity figures.
