Design commitments

What we promise

1

No account to read

Browsing, search and reading are fully available without signing in. Accounts are only needed to save across devices or to publish.

2

No forced ads

Rewarded ads are never shown automatically, and every gated episode also offers a coin route so you can skip advertising entirely.

3

No invented metrics

No fake ratings, read counts, reviews or popularity figures anywhere in the product or on this site.

4

No analytics

No telemetry, no crash reporting, no reading-history profiling. Your reading data stays on your device.

5

Honest consent

Advertising consent is requested before any ad is loaded, states the real trade-off, and can be withdrawn at any time.

6

You can leave

Export everything as JSON, or erase all local data in one action, from inside the app.
